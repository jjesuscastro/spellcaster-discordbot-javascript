✦ COOLDOWNS AND MP DRAIN
33
Each spell has a cooldown as well as an MP cost/drain. Generally speaking, the more powerful the attack, the more MP it uses, the longer the cooldown. When making spells, you have an option to adjust your MP drain (the rest is adjusted via calculations for you)

MP does not regenerate naturally, i.e, a spell must be cast. If a student loses all MP, they resort to basic combat. Please manage your MP wisely!
34
✦ ROLLING
35
To use a spell, students use a combination of their MAG stat + a second stat. The second stat can be seen according to the type of status effect you choose.

MAG is used to determine if a spell is successfully cast. The better your cast, the more magic you channel. To cast a spell successfully, you have to roll above a 10 on a base of d20, with MAG being the modifier.

The second stat determines how powerful the spell is, i.e, the main multiplier for the spell values. Lore wise, MAG is how you channel your magic outward, and the second stat is how well you form it into a spell.

✦ KOs
42
Losing all your HP does not result in perma death, just a knock out and a trip to the infirmary.

Losing all your MP results in a bit of fatigue, colloquially referred to as magic anemia. You will not pass out though! Basic Combat is still available.
